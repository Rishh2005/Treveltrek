## ✨ Features

- **Easy Itinerary Planning**: Create and manage your travel plans effortlessly.
- **Budgeting Made Simple**: Get recommendations tailored to your budget.
- **Real-Time Booking**: Book flights and hotels directly from the app.
- **AI Travel Assistant**: Get personalized travel advice and assistance.
- **Global Currency Support**: Hassle-free currency conversions for transactions.
- **Interactive Maps**: Explore destinations using integrated maps.
- **Immersive Virtual Tours**: Preview destinations with virtual reality tours (future feature).
- **Secure Transactions**: Enjoy a secure platform for all your bookings and payments.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Visit the [TravelTrek Website](https://traveltrekadventures.netlify.app/).
2. Start by entering your travel preferences.
3. Explore tailored recommendations for destinations, accommodations, and activities.
4. Book flights and hotels seamlessly.
5. Enjoy real-time guidance from the AI Travel Assistant during your trip.
6. Access your itinerary anytime, anywhere, with offline support.
